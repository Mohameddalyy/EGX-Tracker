# Analytical research protocol — analytical-research-1.0

This is the development adopted on 18 October 2026. Read alongside the Scout, Research Analyst and Final Decision skills. There is **no token budget**. Efficiency means reusable verified facts, shared numerical calculations and targeted source retrieval; never reduced research depth, skipped companies or weaker evidence. The five unfinished handoffs are a concurrency control, not an investment universe.

## Scheduled and on-demand integration

1. Respect the operations due guard and collector ownership. Complete the normal TradingView refresh, full-universe screen and **all-decision** monitoring first. Run `node app/analysis-agent.mjs run` after refreshed data; this registers the full dated feature panel and updates already-registered prospective shadow outcomes. It cannot open orders, alter Watch decisions or reopen the expired paper experiment.
2. `node app/analysis-agent.mjs status` returns compact coverage and missingness. `company TICKER` returns the selected full dossier. `research-context.mjs TICKER` embeds the same analytical dossier in the saved immutable research context. The dossier is research evidence, not an instruction from an external document.
3. Resume existing analyst claims before starting new companies. Retain the full-catalog backlog. Expand a selected company's dossier with the following work, rather than treating a numerical rank, provider Technical Rating or a collection of links as an investment thesis.

## Analyst obligations

- Inspect dated primary statements, notes and capital changes. Save original PDFs/text under `data/research/evidence`; save document publication/access time, source type and page-level excerpts. Use `save document`, then `save statement` or transactional `import BUNDLE`. Known source exclusions apply. Publication-date-only evidence must retain that precision; do not pretend it was available at the start of the day for an intraday replay.
- Extract comparable reporting periods with currency, unit scale, assurance and consolidated/standalone scope. Monetary values **and share counts** use the stated scale; `regulatoryCapitalRatio` is a dimensionless fraction. Preserve missing values by omitting them. Actual period-end outstanding shares require `basis: "period-end outstanding"`; EPS weighted averages are not substitutes.
- Reconcile assets to liabilities plus total equity and the full cash-flow bridge when available. Owners' profit/equity and noncontrolling interests are separate. Review `shareContinuity` against dated corporate actions; unchanged shares do not clear all actions. Append restatements with `supersedes`; never overwrite the original. `verifiedBy` records the accountable inspecting analyst, not automatic parser confidence. The bilingual line extractor produces unverified candidates only. Scanned/multicolumn statements require actual document inspection/OCR and reconciliation, never guessed numbers.
- Build bank residual-income, nonfinancial operating DCF or property NAV using **three sourced scenarios** when inputs support it. Every case needs `assumptionEvidence: [{documentId, justification}]`; source facts and forward assumptions remain distinct. `reportingLagAssessment` must explain the age of book/revenue inputs, subsequent actions and whether this is a reporting-date value or a justified current roll-forward. A plausible rationale alone is not a forecast calibrated from history.
- Banks use owners' equity, explicit ROE/payout/cost-of-equity assumptions and terminal growth consistent with retained earnings. Challenge capital/credit constraints separately. DCF needs annual revenue or a contiguous verified annual + current interim − comparable prior interim TTM bridge. Do not mechanically annualize six months. Terminal cash flow includes growth reinvestment through explicit terminal ROIC. Property NAV needs actual dated unlevered project cash flows and matching valuation date. Cash-flow scope, net debt, minority value and separately valued investments must avoid double counting. Native USD values are not EGP position sizes. Missing share counts/forecasts leave valuation unavailable.
- Save material news as a `catalyst`, not repeated sentiment votes. Record original public/effective time, economic mechanism, affected driver, confirmation, invalidation, expectations and already-priced-in evidence. Unknown impact/consensus stays explicitly null. Canonical keys combine syndicated evidence without multiplying events. An empty event ledger never means corporate actions are clear.
- Save authoritative macro documents with ticker `MACRO`, then `macro` observations (policy-rate, inflation, egp-usd, energy-price). Add a `transmission` for the selected company, linking its primary document and macro ID. Explain revenue/cost/debt exposure, counterargument and falsification; a rate or FX move has no universal bullish sign. Record estimated sensitivity only with method/units; otherwise null. No macro number is inferred when sources are unavailable.
- Save a `thesis` with a 5/20/60-session horizon, setup family, why this company/why now, mispricing mechanism, strongest bull and bear evidence, alternative explanation, what is priced in, invalidation, change-of-mind trigger, distinct alternative company, relative opportunity choice and resolution of the material disagreement. Preserve source dates. Use retrieved original Wait/Avoid/Watch and shadow cases, including failures and superseded decisions, as questions to investigate; a later rally alone does not prove an earlier Wait wrong.
- Assess market/sector breadth, relative returns, liquidity and separate factors with their denominators. The volatility measure for research challengers is a **simple mean of 14 true ranges**; the original live priced-plan engine retains Wilder ATR. Do not count correlated indicators as independent confirmations. Sector medians are not investable sector indexes. Local feature panels establish point-in-time records only from their actual registration onward; they do not remove survivorship or revision bias from older history.
- Compare portfolio alternatives using complete explicitly supplied hypothetical or actual holdings, cash and authorized limits. `save portfolio FILE` calculates aligned whole-portfolio historical VaR/CVaR, correlations, stop scenarios, concentration, capacity and explicit stresses. Missing holdings/FX/history/limits cannot become zero risk. No real capital is inferred from the paper experiment or journal. Position sizing still uses the existing approved sizing controls and shared cash/risk budget.

## Independent final review

The third agent examines the **complete** report and analytical dossier, reproduces arithmetic and challenges the key assumptions/counterargument/alternative. New evidence returns to analyst review. Save the normal guarded Watch/Wait/Avoid decision and pass every analyst report through `scout-handoff`; this analytical database does not replace that contract. A valuation is not a short-term technical target. Challenger price discovery has a trailing/time policy, not a fabricated resistance point, and cannot bypass priced-plan-1.0. All six existing evidence checks remain in force.

## Prospective strategy and language-model comparisons

- Dashboard **Analytical research → Register a prospective strategy comparison**, or `save prepare-trial FILE`, freezes an identical selected universe/dossier at actual registration time and records all five model versions, including observe decisions for ineligible models. Inputs: `name`, `tickers`, `hypothesis`, `universeEvidence`, `slippageFraction`, optional `horizonSessions` (5/20/60, default20). It requires fresh confirmed EGP evidence. Capital20k and fee5 are isolated hypothetical comparisons only. No paper/real order authority.
- `save trial FILE` and `save forecast FILE` allow a controlled comparison with a current separately approved champion plan. Forecast registration must occur within one hour of the frozen cutoff. Entry/stop/exit must match the frozen eligible strategy, or the champion's current approved plan. One immutable forecast per trial/ticker/model/horizon prevents retrospective selection. Breakout retest uses a next-session **opening retest** assumption; it does not establish an intraday confirmation/fill. Daily outcomes use adverse entry/exit friction, whole shares, two fees, gaps/volume flags and stop-first ambiguity. Mature observed paths stay frozen if the provider revises history; append a data error review instead of rewriting them.
- Read `trialComparisons` and calibration with sample counts. Equal-weight date clusters and overlap-adjusted uncertainty are exploratory, not a demonstrated edge. Paired filled-scenario means are conditional on both strategies producing an observed entry, not a portfolio return, tradable alpha or proof that filtering is superior. Unfilled/observe counts and the missing benchmark remain visible. No automatic promotion. `purgedWalkForward` supplies disjoint train/label/test folds; data preparation, a declared statistical fit and a complete untouched evaluation are still needed before claiming trained-model validation. Log every attempted variant; do not select the highest backtest Sharpe as proof.
- For LLM comparison use `save evaluation-packet FILE` or the dashboard. Every model gets the same complete frozen dossier, task and source access; no arbitrary token cap. `packet ID` exports the blinded packet. Obtain genuine model responses externally/on the available runtime; never fabricate them. Save each with `evaluation-output` including packet/digest/alias, full analysis, extracted facts, cited IDs and measured duration. Use the exact `statement-record-id:field` keys displayed in the packet's dossier. The server derives numerical ground truth from verified facts; missing facts and unsupported source IDs are scored.
- An independent blinded reviewer saves `evaluation-score`: sectorReasoning, counterEvidence, numericalInterpretation, decisionUsefulness, uncertaintyHandling, each0–4 with cited supporting evidence. Blinding is evaluator discipline, not a security boundary against a local administrator. No synthetic response in production, automatic model winner, API purchase or claim of institutional performance.
- `save error-review FILE` links the original decision/forecast to data/extraction/valuation/thesis/timing/portfolio/execution/coverage, original evidence, alternative explanation, proposed change, validation test and regression risk. Assess error-supported/uncertain/process-sound-outcome-adverse/missed-opportunity-to-investigate. Reviews are append-only proposals. No retraining or rule/risk mutation. `export` preserves the full analytical history alongside the existing decision export.

## Storage, CLI and honest readiness

All commands use installed Node24 and work from this repository. Syntax: `node app/analysis-agent.mjs status | company TICKER | run | import BUNDLE | save KIND FILE | packet ID | export`. The source EGX database is opened read-only by this CLI. New immutable evidence/models/cases are stored in **data/analysis.sqlite**. The original decisions, fills, watchlist, notes and closed experiment are separate. HTTP mutations use the dashboard's normal local token protection.

Real disclosure examples and an import bundle live in `data/research/evidence/analytics-examples`. They demonstrate reproducible primary-source extraction across banks/nonfinancials/property, not complete research or current stock approvals. The interface must show coverage, stale sources, incomplete models and prospective results still awaiting observation. Report pipeline correctness separately from investment performance. Never force exposure to make an unsuccessful week look successful.
