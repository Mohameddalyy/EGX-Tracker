### Targeted repair and retained primary evidence

- `node app/diagnose-preparation.mjs` classifies the latest unresolved history attempts without opening charts. Empty charts, wrong symbols, missing completed sessions and conflicting retained tails stay distinct. `node app/reconcile-symbols.mjs` checks exact provider identities; finding a symbol does not verify listing status or tradability. `node app/import-market.mjs --all --failed --retry-unavailable` is an explicit failed-chart repair pass, not an hourly action. No synthesized bars or inferred suspensions.
- `refresh-market.mjs` invokes `reconcile-facts.mjs --tickers=...` only for omitted scanner members. Successful component receipts prevent redoing the full daily scan; targeted reconciliation also reuses its dated receipt. Primary financial verification remains separate from provider identity checks.
- Importers persist the original chart before navigation. A forced child interruption triggers bounded restoration and identity/timeframe/type verification. A live collector retains ownership. A failed restoration stays visible for recovery; do not launch a competing collector.
- Run `python app/inspect-documents.py` after a source collection pass. It hashes raw bytes and reuses extraction only for the matching hash and inspection version. Scanned or image-backed pages remain OCR/layout review work even if a PDF header extracts as text. `discovered-routes.json` contains observed links for the next explicit collection batch; do not recursively crawl it without reviewing scope. `collect-documents.mjs --routes=<saved-route-file>` resumes that batch. `--repair` permits an explicit bounded retry of transient/oversized responses; missing routes and denied access retain their separate backoff.
- `node app/collect-exchange.mjs --from=YYYY-MM-DD --to=YYYY-MM-DD` retains the public EGX news and financial-statement feeds with page checkpoints, raw requests/responses, content versions and explicit pagination coverage. Without a date window it resumes/reuses the latest window, then collects the new interval with a two-calendar-day overlap. `--refresh` explicitly archives and restarts a window when pagination changed or older revisions require checking. Page completeness covers only the requested categories/window; it is not all-event clearance. Current-session updates require an explicit `--to` because the default ends yesterday in Cairo.
- Reviewed PDF facts require source hash, issuer identity, fiscal period, page, unit/scale, accounting basis, method and limitations through `saveDocumentReview`. They are appended to the evidence library and selected research contexts. Exchange contexts include exact tickers and unambiguous retained ISIN associations as unreviewed evidence. Conflicting or shared identities are quarantined in identityReview; unmatched announcements remain in the full index. Association does not certify identity or clear a corporate action. No document download, scanner recovery or feed completion creates a decision or trade approval.
