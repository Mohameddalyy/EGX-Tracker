# Research foundation — checked 8 October 2026

These are published institutional approaches, not proprietary bank trading systems or evidence of profitability on EGX. The machine screen is a deliberately simple local triage policy. Its exact thresholds and binary weights need forward evaluation, transaction-cost stress and point-in-time data before performance claims.

| Published source | Supported concept | Local implementation and limits |
|---|---|---|
| [AQR: Value and Momentum Everywhere](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Journal-Article/Value-and-Momentum-Everywhere) | Value and momentum offer distinct information across markets. | Compare price persistence with industry-relative valuation; a low P/E alone is insufficient. No claim that this EGX screen reproduces AQR returns. |
| [MSCI Momentum methodology](https://www.msci.com/indexes/documents/methodology/2_MSCI_Momentum_Indexes_Methodology_20231120.pdf) | Medium-horizon risk-adjusted momentum excluding the latest month. | Six-month return using 126 observations and skipping 21; top-30% rank of eligible local shares. This approximation does not implement MSCI's calendar-month, risk-free-rate or three-year weekly-volatility methodology. |
| [MSCI Quality](https://www.msci.com/indexes/group/quality-indexes) | Profitability, earnings stability and leverage matter. | Screen on peer-relative ROE where enough peers exist; analyst checks earnings stability and balance sheet. No bank debt/equity cutoff borrowed from industrials. |
| [J.P. Morgan Asset Management equity research](https://am.jpmorgan.com/us/en/asset-management/institutional/media/global-equities-research-capabilities/) | Company quality, risk and cash-flow-based valuation with comparable research. | Independent analyst review of business, financial statements, governance, catalysts and bear case before watchlist promotion. |
| [AQR trading costs research](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Working-Paper/Trading-Costs-of-Asset-Pricing-Anomalies) | Costs and capacity affect implementability. | Median daily close × volume proxy, recent nonzero-volume participation and ATR limits; this is not executed turnover, spread or an order-book model. |

The screen gives one point each for close above SMA50; positive six-month momentum excluding 21 observations; top-30% momentum; positive P/E below eligible industry median; positive ROE above industry median; three-month TradingView snapshot performance above EGX30; and confirmed RV20 >= 1.5. Industry comparisons require at least five valid peers. Three points passes the research threshold; strongest candidates are queued first. Scores are correlated heuristics, not independent evidence or calibrated probabilities.

Local eligibility assumptions: at least 148 confirmed bars, capture <=36 hours old, last session <=7 calendar days old, at least 18 positive-volume observations of the last 20, median close×volume >=EGP1m, ATR14/close <=10%. USD listings are retained in coverage but deferred until FX-aware liquidity/sizing exists. These are configurable engineering/research starting values, not universal institutional rules or user-approved portfolio risk limits.

EGX30 and EGX70EWI snapshot performance provide large-cap and broader-market context. The snapshot can include an unfinished session; it is not silently merged into confirmed daily OHLCV. The analyst must distinguish price returns, total returns and EGP/FX-adjusted returns. Missing values stay missing. Suspensions, holidays, stale financial periods, related-party transactions, one-off earnings and dilution require explicit investigation.

The initial scout-1.0 run used broad sector peers. Analyst review identified that TradingView Finance mixes banks, holdings and property developers; scout-1.1 uses industry instead. Old queued evidence remains unchanged and must not be treated as a reliable valuation comparison. Industry categories remain imperfect; analysts verify true business comparability.
