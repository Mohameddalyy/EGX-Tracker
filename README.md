# EGX Tracker — local research desk

The **EGX Decision Scout** (`$egx-scout`) now screens market coverage, records an auditable candidate queue, and hands candidates to a separate **EGX Research Analyst**. The scheduled chat runs at **22:00 Africa/Cairo, Sunday–Thursday**, with a full catalog/history retry on Sunday. The computer, Codex and TradingView need to be available for local collection. The app schedule is `egx-decision-scout-and-research-analyst`; manage it through Codex Scheduled. Watchlist promotion and notification require a complete analyst report; passing the numerical screen alone never adds a stock or places a trade.

Open **Decision Scout** in the dashboard to inspect the queue, scores, reasons, gaps, analyst outcomes and unread notifications. The manual “Screen stored data” button only queues candidates; ask Codex to run `$egx-scout` to execute the analyst handoff immediately. The schedule invokes the full workflow. Use “Reload watchlist and reports” after additions. Your original manual notes, holdings and pause settings are preserved.

`research/screening-policy.json` holds transparent local thresholds. The screen follows published principles from AQR, MSCI and J.P. Morgan Asset Management; the numerical policy is experimental, not their proprietary strategy or a validated EGX edge. See `agent/egx-scout/references/benchmarks.md`. Version 1.1 corrects broad Finance-sector comparisons to finer industry peers. Initial 1.0 queue evidence remains immutable and is independently challenged by the analyst.

TradingView daily OHLCV, financial snapshots and EGX30/EGX70EWI performance are connected through `app/collect.mjs` and `app/collect-facts.mjs`. Availability is not universal: initial P/E coverage is 50/296 listings. `research/tradingview-capabilities.json` records verified routes, missing capabilities and limitations. Ownership and news use TradingView pages and issuer/EGX disclosures where the MCP has no dedicated tool. Do not infer ownership from float or volume. All material data gaps remain visible and can prevent watchlist promotion.

For operators: `node app/scout-run.mjs` screens stored data; `node app/scout-handoff.mjs queue` reads pending work; `claim ID` starts a review; `apply ID REPORT_ID` applies a saved analyst decision with validation and idempotent notifications; `fail "Reason"` records a blocked run. Reports and contexts remain under `data/research`, market facts/raw snapshots under `data/market`, and queue/history/notifications in `data/egx.sqlite`. Portfolio limits remain unset until supplied by the user.


EGX Research Analyst is installed as the Codex skill `$egx-research` (maintained source in `agent/egx-research`). Invoke it in Codex to perform an on-demand sourced watchlist/holdings review. The agent prioritizes swing and medium-term trades, assesses entries/stops/targets and per-share/percentage risk before portfolio capital is supplied, then uses user-approved portfolio limits for share sizing. It uses available TradingView MCP capabilities and web research, and must report unavailable tools/data. The Scout schedule invokes the analyst automatically. No account subscription changes or trading execution are configured.

Read `agent/egx-research/references/research-method.md` for the public institutional research foundation. It distinguishes J.P. Morgan's fundamental process, AQR's factor/cost research and BlackRock's portfolio-risk principles from our own prototype rules. The agent has no proprietary bank model access. `research/profile.json` intentionally leaves capital/cash/risk limits unset.

Run `node app/research-context.mjs` to freeze local watchlist, data quality and actual positions. The model uses that context plus opened external sources to draft its report, then `node app/research-save.mjs path-to-draft.json` validates and saves it without overwriting older reports. Agent research in the dashboard displays saved reports and limitations. The initial readiness report is setup verification, not a completed company review. `entryRisk` and `sizeCandidate` in `app/research.mjs` provide deterministic scenario calculations; share sizing stays blocked when required user inputs are absent.

Actual positions: within a saved journal plan, expand “Record an actual fill.” Enter Buy/Sell, Cairo fill date, whole shares, price per share, total EGP fees and an optional broker reference. Buys add weighted-average cost including fees; sells allocate that cost proportionally and deduct sell fees from realized profit/loss. Partial exits retain the remaining cost basis. Overselling, invalid dates and backdated fills after later records are rejected. Correct the latest active fill by voiding it with a reason; the original remains in the export/audit history. Correct older fills in reverse order before re-entering them. These records are manual, not broker orders or verified executions.

The position panel shows holdings, average buy price, remaining cost, realized/unrealized P/L and risk to the saved stop. Risk is max(0, remaining cost minus shares × stop); target profit is shares × target minus remaining cost. Estimates exclude future exit fees/slippage. Marks use the latest confirmed stored close on or after the latest active fill date; otherwise valuation is unavailable. Stale capture labels remain visible. After a TradingView refresh, use “Reload positions and prices” to update journal marks (unsaved fill forms will be reset). Corporate actions/dividends need manual reconciliation; reviews and reported closed statuses do not change the fill ledger. JSON journal export includes all fills, void reasons and computed positions.

Attention workflow: Refresh TradingView, review the “Attention today” queue, inspect supporting numbers/dates, open the stock or linked journal plan, then mark the item reviewed. Reviewed items persist in SQLite; changed evidence gets a new key, and inactive items remain in History. Reopen reverses an acknowledgement. Rules: confirmed close within 0.5 ATR14 of support/resistance; confirmed RV20 >= 2; capture older than 24 hours; unconfirmed dates; provider-calendar gaps; recorded import failures; and saved-plan stop/target touches on sessions strictly after plan creation. Paused stocks and terminal journal plans are excluded. Provisional plan observations are labelled and are not verified executions. Stale captures suppress all market/plan signals until refreshed. This panel evaluates on dashboard loads and refresh completion; it does not schedule background collection or send notifications. Retained import errors describe past failures, including failures followed by successful imports.

Watchlist management: open “Manage stocks” below the watchlist to add an EGX ticker and optional label, or pause/resume collection. The active watchlist has no fixed 20-stock cap; collection uses sequential batches of eight. Pausing preserves history, notes and journal entries. Refresh collects the active database list, checks EGX identity/currency/OHLCV/adjustment settings, and imports each valid stock independently. A failed symbol remains pending or retains its old capture, with the import error in market coverage. The collector restores the original chart; timeouts can interrupt restoration. New stocks have no signals until enough confirmed history is available. The initial expansion adds TMGH, HRHO and ETEL as coverage examples, not recommendations.

Decision journal: select a stock and choose “Draft plan for selected stock.” Review entry, stop and target; enter a horizon, thesis and invalidation condition. Saving preserves the original plan plus current price history, capture metadata, indicators and levels. Later reviews append manual statuses and notes without changing the plan. These are research records, not broker orders or verified fills. JSON export includes full evidence and review history. Journal records are kept in the same local SQLite database and included in its backup. Notion sync remains unconnected.

Journal QA: 15 unit tests pass. An isolated in-memory server exercised real browser/API saves, reviews, export data, draft stock persistence, token protection, escaped notes and desktop/mobile layouts. To repeat, launch with `EGX_DATABASE=:memory:` and run `app/journal-browser-check.mjs` with `EGX_PLAYWRIGHT` set to the installed Playwright module. Never run the write-based browser test against a personal journal.

Historical replay v1.0 is available below price levels. Starting after 50 observations, each stop method independently enters at the next observed open if it lies between the fixed stop and nearest resistance target. One position per method; exits on stop, target or 20 holding bars. Both levels touched means stop first. Stop gaps fill at open; favourable target gaps are capped at target. Incomplete horizons are omitted. Evaluation windows crossing known gaps/unconfirmed bars are skipped. Illustrative fee/slippage is 0.25% per side. Returns assume full reinvestment; drawdown uses closed trades only. This is an exploratory rule test using revised historical provider data, not a validated strategy or point-in-time dataset. Inspect individual outcomes in the dashboard.

Run `./start-tracker.ps1` in PowerShell, then open http://127.0.0.1:4317. Requires Node 24+ (the launcher uses the installed Codex runtime). Stop with Ctrl+C. Only binds to this computer.

The dashboard initially covered COMI, AMOC and SWDY; the watchlist is now configurable. On first launch it imports the dated verification capture into `data/egx.sqlite`. The capture timestamp is preserved; saved history is not represented as a fresh live fetch. CSV export includes source daily bars, including any current-date provisional bar.

Refresh requires TradingView Desktop running with `--remote-debugging-port=9222 --remote-debugging-address=127.0.0.1` and an open candlestick chart, plus the installed bottrading connector in `integrations/tradingview-mcp`. Refresh temporarily switches symbols/timeframes and restores the original chart. Do not interact with the chart during refresh. Failures preserve the affected stock history and appear in the activity log; other valid stocks can still import. A forced timeout can interrupt restoration; check your chart if that happens.

Data policy: TradingView primary; 15-minute delay currently verified. Raw capture payloads are preserved per successful run; current bars are upserted by ticker/session. Missing values and adjustment changes cause rejection. Dividend adjustment must remain off. Provider split handling is retained without a second adjustment. Provider holidays inform gap flags but are not independently certified. Same-day bars require persisted capture-time evidence: verified EGX daytime metadata and holidays, the later of 14:30 Cairo or the provider close, feed delay and a 30-minute buffer. Unknown metadata stays provisional. Later-date recaptures remain eligible. Elapsed time alone never promotes an earlier intraday capture. Per-bar capture timestamps are migrated from matching saved run payloads; missing provenance stays unconfirmed. No automatic orders, signal qualification, fundamental scoring or scheduled refresh.

Indicators v1.0: SMA20/SMA50 on available accepted daily closes; RV20 uses the median of the preceding 20 volumes; ATR14 uses Wilder smoothing with 14 initial true ranges. These are observation-based calculations; review date gaps before using affected windows. Captures older than 24 hours receive a freshness warning, not a claim about exchange session completeness.

Notion is not connected in this version. Recommended future scope: research notes, evidence links, watchlist summaries and decision records. Keep OHLCV and calculations here; use stable IDs and explicit sync direction before adding Notion writes.

Back up the `data` directory with the server stopped. It contains the local SQLite database, watchlist notes, capture history and edit log. Market data is private and not deployed or republished.

Price levels v1.0: the last 120 accepted daily observations produce strict swing highs/lows confirmed by two observations on each side. Pivots broken by a later close are discarded. Surviving pivots cluster within 0.5 ATR14 total span; the three closest zones on each side of the reference close are shown with dated evidence. Long-position stop candidates use support lower edge minus 0.5 ATR14 and reference close minus 2 ATR14. Reward/risk uses nearest resistance and the same reference close, not an actual entry. No backtesting, tick-size rounding, execution guarantee or automatic orders. Daily settings are independent of assigned investment horizon. Method details and data-quality caveats appear in the panel.

Tests: `npm test` (or `node --test app/core.test.mjs app/levels.test.mjs`). No application packages are required beyond Node; TradingView collection uses the separately installed connector dependencies.




Full-market coverage: **Explore EGX** searches the TradingView stock catalog by ticker, company and sector. **Add to watchlist** makes a catalog stock available in the research desk and in the agent’s next context; **Resume** reactivates a paused stock. Stored market history is separate from the personal watchlist, and adding stocks remains available during a market import. USD listings retain USD units; EGP-only journal plans and sizing are blocked for those stocks.

`node app/discover-market.mjs` refreshes `data/market/catalog.json` from TradingView’s Egypt stock scanner, checking that all reported rows were received. `node app/import-market.mjs` registers the catalog and imports pending/failed histories; `--all` refreshes every catalog stock and `--watchlist` refreshes active watched stocks. The dashboard provides both market-history actions and displays progress/failure reasons. One chart job runs at a time. Run discovery again to pick up new listings; the saved catalog is not refreshed automatically. This is TradingView coverage, including inactive securities, not an official listing reconciliation. Up to 500 available daily bars per capture are retained, not complete lifetime histories or live exchange trade counts.

For research, `node app/research-context.mjs` captures the active watchlist plus journal evidence and market coverage; pass tickers such as `ABUK COMI` for selected research or `--all` for a large full-market context. Context generation includes paused holdings, preserves currency, and rejects unknown symbols. Adding to the watchlist does not launch research automatically: invoke **Use $egx-research to review my watchlist and holdings.** in Codex, then reload reports in the dashboard.


Current automated workflow (October 2026): Scout → Research Analyst → Final Decision Agent → watchlist/outcome history. The existing 22:00 Africa/Cairo Sunday–Thursday heartbeat runs the three roles and checks every recorded decision, including Wait/Avoid. This supersedes earlier prototype descriptions of manual-only refresh. Codex, the local computer and TradingView must be available; data/tool failures remain visible.

`node app/scout-handoff.mjs apply CANDIDATE_ID REPORT_ID` now hands the saved report to the decision inbox only. `$egx-decision` independently challenges research and saves Watch, Wait or Avoid via `node app/decision-agent.mjs save FILE`. Watch requires the complete, current analyst-approved evidence and risk plan. No actual order or assumed fill occurs. See `agent/egx-decision/references/contract.md` and dashboard `/#decisions`.

`node app/decision-agent.mjs monitor` appends forward observations at 5/20/60 observed sessions using the first confirmed session open strictly after the decision date. It continues after those horizons and after supersession. Wait/Avoid stocks are included in `import-market.mjs --known` even if not watched. Missing sessions block forecast scoring. Daily stop/target touches cannot establish entry or ordering. No total-return/benchmark alpha claim. Fresh captures are needed; elapsed time does not finalize an old intraday bar.

All decision reasoning, original report/context, rule versions, forecasts and subsequent observations are preserved in SQLite and exportable through `/api/decisions/export` or `node app/decision-agent.mjs export`. A future fourth reviewer can use this evidence to propose and test improvements. This implementation does not retrain model weights, automatically tune thresholds or treat price direction as proof of decision correctness. No forward results are backfilled before a decision. Manual notes, pauses and actual holdings remain separate.


Post-close daily data (8 October 2026): `research/session-policy.json` records the 14:30 Cairo regular close verified against TradingView metadata and Thndr's published Main/SME session table (https://support.thndr.app/en/articles/638525-why-is-my-stock-order-still-pending). The direct EGX session page was inaccessible. At ingestion, the tracker uses the later of this floor and supported provider session/correction end times, adds the actual feed delay and a 30-minute buffer, and saves the proof in `bar_captures.finality`. For the current 900-second feed, the regular cutoff is 15:15 Cairo; Egypt's seasonal UTC offset is handled by Africa/Cairo. Known holidays/weekends, missing current-year holiday data, timezone/delay/session gaps and unsupported formats cannot qualify a same-day capture. Dated exchange overrides may delay or block eligibility; shorter sessions never lower the verified floor. Review source guidance when schedules change.

Snapshot indicators, support/resistance, screener, research contexts and outcome monitoring share this proof. Earlier same-day captures and legacy captures lacking proof are never upgraded just because time passes; refresh them after the cutoff or on a later date. Research contexts and decision histories already saved remain immutable. Outcome monitoring may include today's accepted close for decisions from earlier dates; it still excludes the decision's own date to avoid attributing pre-decision movement to an outcome. All original outcome rows remain available.


Priced conditional buys: new Watch approvals now require decision-1.1 and a complete priced-plan-1.0 plan generated by `node app/trade-plan.mjs CONTEXT_ID TICKER pullback|breakout ZONE_INDEX EXPIRY COST_FRACTION "Cost assumption"`. Entry zone, stop, up to two measured resistance targets, pivot evidence, tick rounding, gross/cost-adjusted risk and triggers are recomputed from frozen context bars. Price edits fail validation. The initial 2:1 cost-adjusted T1 gate, 0.25-ATR entry width and 0.5-ATR stop buffer are local unvalidated research heuristics. Missing targets or weak geometry requires deferral. See `agent/egx-research/references/priced-plans.md` for the full contract and sources.

Final-decision cards show the priced setup and forward daily condition/level observations. “Record my actual trades” creates an idempotent linked journal, with no fills or orders. The user records actual quantities/prices/fees using the existing fill ledger; position/P&L then appears on the decision card. Setup observations do not infer fills or intraday order. Daily monitoring is not an intraday stop service. New plan prices require new analyst evidence and a superseding decision; historical plans remain unchanged.
