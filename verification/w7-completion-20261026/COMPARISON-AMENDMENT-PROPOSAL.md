# W7 comparison amendment proposal — not activated

This is a proposed evaluation protocol, not a change to signal thresholds, FULL risk discretion, frozen exits, or the completed experiment. No scheduled run, entry cohort or real trade is authorized by this document. Values below are proposed design choices, not empirically estimated optimal thresholds.

## Economic decision and stopping

1. Retain all 18 arms, starting each independently at EGP 100,000, and the 16 primary FULL-versus-comparator contrasts (eight comparisons per cost scenario). Do not pool base and stress results. Preserve failed and unavailable arms.
2. Register one fixed 126-exchange-session observation endpoint before the first authorized cohort. Entry cohorts within that window require separate explicit authorization; the initial five-session entry pilot does not authorize continuing entries. End economic measurement at session 126 even if positions remain open: report reconciled marked equity, pending exits and liquidation sensitivity, never assume a terminal fill.
3. Require at least 100 appropriate closed observations per comparison and sufficient independent cohorts/precision. Failure to reach the floor, missing daily equity or unresolved actions yields INCONCLUSIVE; no selective endpoint extension. Safety incidents can stop new entries early; retain monitoring and report the interrupted trial as such. Do not repeatedly test for significance during the window. Routine operational and risk reporting continues.
4. Proposed minimum material benefit is EGP 1,000 additional net marked wealth (1% of starting capital) versus each relevant comparator at the endpoint. This terminal-wealth hurdle is distinct from the existing paired mean-daily-return estimator. Both the material hurdle and positive paired-return evidence must be met for that contrast: the fixed dependence-aware interval must exclude zero and the centered-null test must survive Holm adjustment across all 16 hypotheses. A passed contrast is not universal model superiority; disclose all other contrasts and block-length sensitivity. These conditions are necessary, not sufficient, and do not override poor precision or dependent observations.
5. Risk acceptance is a separate human decision. Require zero hard-control violations, zero unexplained accounting differences, explicit drawdown, concentration, scenario loss, time underwater, turnover and liquidation-delay comparisons. Higher profit accompanied by greater risk is a tradeoff, not an automatic win. This adds no allocation, concentration, or loss ceiling to FULL; its justified paper discretion remains intact. No result can automatically promote a policy or authorize real money.
6. Unavailable metrics stay unavailable. Missing return days are not removed to shorten the horizon, forward-filled, or bridged as one-day returns. No entitlement or cash yield is guessed. Cash comparator remains unremunerated cash unless a separate sourced interest convention is registered.

## Attainable buy-and-hold comparator proposal

This comparator is additional descriptive context, outside the fixed 16 primary tests. It cannot be added retrospectively after seeing the cohort's outcome.

- Freeze eligible ordinary EGP primary common shares at the prior verified session's close, using the same identity, action, rules, data and liquidity admission controls. Rank by median raw traded value over the previous 20 complete verified sessions; descending value, ISIN ascending as a fixed tie-break. Select five names. Fewer than five admitted names makes this five-name comparator unavailable rather than silently changing its mandate.
- Allocate at most EGP 20,000 cash commitment per name, inclusive of entry cost and exit-fee reserve; whole shares only. Set each limit at the prior raw close rounded down to its verified legal tick, within verified daily bands. Use the first session's frozen 30-minute window and the same delayed-minute, full-fill, capacity, slippage, fee and settlement machinery. This is a deliberately feasible limit-order buy-and-hold comparator; missed entry windows remain cash, with no replacement or hindsight purchase.
- Hold fills through the fixed 126-session endpoint without the strategy's discretionary/technical exits. Corporate-action identity, entitlement and valuation integrity remain mandatory. Report actual sourced/reconciled dividends; missing entitlement or adjustment makes the affected total-return result unavailable. Do not guess dividends or treat price-only results as total return.
- Freeze instrument selection, prices, rule/source hashes and pending-order rules before entry. Mark the endpoint on verified same-period closes, separately showing a conservative liquidation scenario. Do not describe index points as tradable holdings or compare an executable costed portfolio against an assumed free index fill.
