# Unapproved comparison choices

These proposals are review inputs. They do not amend the frozen plan or activate a cohort.

## Attainable buy-and-hold comparator

Proposed design: at the same pre-entry cutoff, select ordinary EGP stocks from the admitted full universe using a fixed prior-liquidity ranking, independently of future returns and agent recommendations. Freeze basket size, position weights, cash remainder, executable participation/order restrictions, cost assumptions, settlement and dividend/rights treatment. Use the same actual entry window and minute fill constraints; failed fills remain cash and disclosed exclusions. Hold to a fixed registered endpoint, with only mandatory tradability/corporate-action handling. Reconcile entitlements before reporting total return. No hindsight substitutions. This comparator must have its own ledger and source bindings, not a fee-free index rebased to EGP100,000.

Unresolved choices: basket size/weights, entry-window expiry, endpoint and entitlement implementation. None is approved or populated automatically. Price-index context and idle cash are available without inventing this comparator.

## Meaningful benefit, acceptable risk and stopping

The frozen minimum 126 sessions and 100 appropriate closed observations is an inference eligibility floor, not success. Before a prospective cohort, define the economic return improvement worth the additional costs/complexity, tolerable realized drawdown/concentration/exit-delay outcomes, and the exact scheduled analysis endpoint. Record safety-stop criteria and treatment of terminated/unavailable arms. Record all planned hypotheses and data vintages. Freeze any extension as a new authorized cohort; do not keep extending the same test until significance appears.

For FULL versus FULL-BOUNDED, judge the combined allocation/early-risk-exit mandate and the risk consequences. Higher return with materially higher risk does not prove that unrestricted risk is better. A utility/risk preference would be needed for that claim. The user's unrestricted justified **paper** risk authority remains intact; no new binding risk limit is inferred here.

## Fixed-exit diagnostic

Required implementation remains open. The diagnostic must replay desired entry budgets with mandatory exits only, suppress discretionary earlier risk exits, preserve original per-intent quantity ceilings, and recompute available cash, unsettled proceeds, participation, reservations and fills. Report denied/divergent later entries and conditional attribution. It is not a tenth strategy, a copied original portfolio path or newly generated risk advice. The current execution certificate binds the original portfolio snapshot; modifying its fields would create a false approval. Implement this as an explicitly isolated mechanical counterfactual with audited original-intent provenance, not a bypass in the production risk path.
