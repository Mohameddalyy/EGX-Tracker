# EGX v5 data dictionary and gap policy

**Design proposal, 23 October 2026. Not activated.** Covers all 43 probed scanner columns plus 35 additional analytical, execution and evaluation fields.

Criticality is contextual: research discovery, thesis approval, execution and evaluation have different requirements. A missing optional consensus target cannot block a properly evidenced company thesis. Missing current prices, corporate-action reconciliation, authorization or settlement cannot be cured by a score penalty. The existing production approval rules remain unchanged until reviewed implementation.

## Consequence policy

- **C — critical:** block new BUY approval/fill in the affected strategy or instrument. Continue independent research, report the exact blocker and monitor existing exposure. Missing data never authorizes an invented SELL fill.
- **F — conditional critical:** required when a quality/value claim relies on it. A technical research lead can proceed without that feature, but cannot claim completed fundamental approval. Material solvency, reporting or corporate-action doubt is always critical.
- **I — important:** proposed for Phase 3 testing: if unresolved after ordered fallbacks and not material to thesis validity, cap quantity at **50% of the otherwise permitted size** across all important gaps combined. Do not multiply successive penalties. Keep the uncertainty explicit. If this makes the position uneconomic, NO-TRADE. This is an explicit design choice, not empirically optimized or currently authorized.
- **N — nice-to-have:** omit. Do not silently redistribute its score weight to other factors; preserve missingness. No size penalty.
- A field marked not-applicable needs a sector/strategy reason. “Unknown” is not N/A. A fallback value of equivalent quality has no missing-field penalty; a proxy retains its stated limitations.
- Conflicting material values are not averaged away. Reconcile accounting period, revision, unit, share class and original source. If unresolved, use C for a thesis/execution-critical conflict.
- Confidence is a verbal evidence judgment until calibrated. Never subtract arbitrary points from a probability or invent precise success odds.

**Stop the fallback chain** when an acceptable source resolves the field; otherwise preserve the failed attempts and a scheduled repair. Permission-blocked/manual steps are pending, not verified. For a critical unresolved field, produce a conditional thesis and explicit NO-TRADE rather than abandoning the whole company or blocking unrelated candidates.

## Source authority and timing

Market discovery starts with TradingView. Primary issuer/exchange/regulator documents control financial statements, ownership and corporate actions. Browser/provider summaries are discovery aids; the original document must substantiate a critical fact. Manually supplied official material is allowed only after identity, date, completeness and page checks; a screenshot of a number alone is insufficient.

TradingView routes and official-source access were tested in [the capability map](CAPABILITIES-AND-RULES.md). “Official equivalent” in a fallback chain is a designed route, not a claim that every issuer/history endpoint was successfully tested. Do not replace any missing field with the excluded providers.

Every value carries instrument ID, unit/scale/currency, economic period, source URL/document hash/page, publication time if known, effective time, capture time, adjustment basis, extraction version, verification status and expiry. Keep original and restated facts. Unknown publication time means **not point-in-time safe** even if downloaded today.

Freshness uses the expected completed exchange session and effective events. No silent stale reuse. Financial statements need period/release recency, not a 36-hour age limit on the underlying accounts. News has a coverage interval and feed watermark; “no new event found in successfully checked sources” is defensible, “there is no news” is not.

## Gap-log schema and example

Each gap record must include: run/snapshot/instrument/field IDs; detected time; last valid observation and expected freshness; required scope/criticality; ordered attempts with URL, result, error and time; chosen value/source/proxy formula and units; alternative/conflicting values; missing consequence; before/after eligibility, sizing and evidence confidence; owner, next attempt, deadline and resolution reference. No overwrite of earlier failed evidence.

Example from this phase: SAIB history retrieval succeeded, but the newest bar was 15 September and the native currency was USD. Log two separate fields: historical freshness and currency/strategy eligibility. For an EGP-only proposed strategy, disposition is NO-TRADE/INELIGIBLE_CURRENCY; independently, its historical series is stale for current monitoring. Do not “refresh” the old price by assigning today's capture time. RMTV's chart failure and absence from today's scanner require a catalog/source repair, not a delisting assertion.

Example of a permissible bypass: absent analyst consensus is N and has no effect on a company-specific valuation derived from primary statements. Example of a conditional bypass: fewer than five valid peers prevents a peer-cheapness claim; a separately substantiated standalone valuation may continue with the important-gap limitation. Missing unresolved capital-action terms cannot be bypassed.

## Derived calculation checks

- Daily returns use one coherent adjustment vintage. Portfolio cash dividends and share changes use the ledger's actual entitlement/corporate-action rules; do not double-count adjusted returns and cash distributions.
- RV20 excludes the current bar from its denominator. Intraday volume compares like clock intervals and session types; Ramadan profiles cannot be mixed blindly.
- Liquidity participation limits apply to shares and value, including the current observed interval. Whole-share rounding and fees are applied before final risk approval.
- Prices must be tick-valid on the relevant side and inside approved zones after rounding. Recalculate stop loss and costs after rounding.
- Missing history cannot be filled with interpolated prices for signal or execution testing. Zero-trade observations, missing observations and exchange-closed dates are distinct.
- Portfolio sizing is constrained by capital, cash, stop-risk, name/sector/group caps and liquidity simultaneously. A planned stop is not a guaranteed maximum loss under gaps or price limits.
- Benchmark horizons are aligned to actual decision/entry timestamps and trading sessions; no mixing of calendar month performance with a five-session experiment.
